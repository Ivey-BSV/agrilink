## Full (for stores that allow longer notes)

**What’s in this release**

CAP (Collective Action Program) helps farmers and producers connect, learn, and act together for regenerative agriculture and a more resilient food system.

**Events**  
Browse and create events—workshops, field days, and meetups. Register, see who’s attending, and share events in chat.

**Community & feed**  
Post updates, photos, and questions. Comment, like, and stay in touch with the community.

**Farm directory**  
Explore a directory of farms and producers. View profiles, specialties, and contact information.

**Chat**  
Message other members one-to-one. Share events and coordinate directly in conversation.

**Collaboration**  
- **Goals** — Set and track personal and community goals for sustainability.  
- **Reciprocity ring** — Post asks and offers to give and get help.  
- **Workshops** — Join workshops and collective action designed with the CAP research team.  
- **Future visualization** — Explore and contribute to shared visions for the future.

**Resources**  
Access news, grants, podcasts, and tools to support regenerative and sustainable farming.

**Marketplace**  
List and discover equipment, produce, and services within the CAP network.

**Profiles & settings**  
Build your profile, manage followers, and control account and privacy settings.

Thank you for joining CAP. Together we can strengthen our farms and the broader food system.
